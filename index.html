Welcome to the Solar System

An interactive, to-scale-ish 3D model. Everything is clickable and zoomable — including every moon.

  • Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Click a planet/moon (or use the list) to fly to it and track it. Scroll while tracking to zoom in/out.
  • Esc releases the camera. Space pauses. 1–9 jumps to planets.
  • Guided tour flies through the system while narrating a story (uses your browser's voice).
  • Realistic scale shrinks planets to true relative sizes (they get tiny — fun to compare).
  • Each body's card has a "Look for" hint so you can identify it by sight.
